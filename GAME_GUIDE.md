# 🎮 Complete Game Guide

## 🌟 Welcome, Adventurer!

This guide will help you master the dungeon and become a legendary hero.

---

## 📖 Story & Setting

You are an adventurer drawn to the **Infinite Dungeon**, a mysterious labyrinth that extends endlessly into the depths of the earth. Legends speak of powerful artifacts and ancient beings trapped within. Many have entered seeking glory and treasure. Few have returned.

The deeper you descend, the more dangerous it becomes. But with danger comes reward - legendary weapons wielded by heroes of old, and the chance to face the epic beings that guard them.

**Your Goal**: Descend as deep as you can, defeat legendary bosses, collect mythical weapons, and become the greatest adventurer in history.

## 🎯 Getting Started

### Character Creation
1. Enter your adventurer name
2. Click "Begin Adventure"
3. You spawn on Floor 1

### Starting Stats
- **Level**: 1
- **HP**: 100
- **STR**: 10 (Physical damage)
- **DEX**: 10 (Dodge chance)
- **INT**: 10 (Magic damage)
- **VIT**: 10 (Defense & HP)

## 🕹️ Controls & Interface

### Movement
- **W** or **↑** - Move Up
- **S** or **↓** - Move Down
- **A** or **←** - Move Left
- **D** or **→** - Move Right

### Combat
- **Click on Enemy** - Attack (must be adjacent)
- Enemies automatically counter-attack
- Combat continues until enemy or player is defeated

### Interface Buttons
- **Skills** - Open skill upgrade menu
- **Inventory** - View and equip items
- **View Legendary Weapons** - Browse all legendary items
- **View Epic Bosses** - Browse all boss lore

---

## ⚔️ Combat System

### Basic Combat
1. Move adjacent to an enemy (diagonal counts)
2. Click the enemy to attack
3. Damage is calculated based on your stats and weapon
4. Enemy counter-attacks if still alive
5. Repeat until victory or defeat

### Damage Calculation
```
Your Damage = (STR × 2) + Weapon Damage + Skill Bonuses
Enemy Damage = Enemy Base Damage - Your Defense
```

### Critical Hits
- **Base Chance**: 10%
- **Effect**: 2x damage
- **Increase**: +5% per level of Critical Eye skill

### Dodging
- **Base Chance**: 5%
- **Effect**: Completely avoid damage
- **Increase**: +3% per level of Quick Reflexes skill

### Lifesteal
- **Requires**: Life Drain skill
- **Effect**: Heal for % of damage dealt
- **Amount**: 10% per skill level

## 📊 Leveling & Progression

### Experience Points (XP)
- Gain XP by defeating enemies
- **Enemy XP**: Level × 25
- **Boss XP**: Level × 50

### Leveling Up
When you gain enough XP:
- **All Stats**: +2 (STR, DEX, INT, VIT)
- **Max HP**: +20
- **HP**: Fully restored
- **Skill Points**: +3

### XP Requirements
- Level 1→2: 100 XP
- Level 2→3: 150 XP
- Level 3→4: 225 XP
- Formula: Previous × 1.5 (exponential)

## 🎓 Skills System

You earn **3 skill points** per level. Spend them wisely!

### Offensive Skills

- **Effect**: +5 damage per level
- **Best For**: All builds
- **Max Benefit**: Unlimited
- **Recommendation**: ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

- **Effect**: +5% crit chance per level
- **Best For**: High-damage builds

- **Recommendation**: ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

#### Arcane Knowledge
- **Effect**: +5 magic damage per level
- **Best For**: Future magic builds
- **Max Benefit**: Unlimited
- **Recommendation**: ⭐⭐⭐ (future-proof)

### Defensive Skills

#### Iron Skin
- **Effect**: +3 defense per level
- **Best For**: Tank builds
- **Max Benefit**: Unlimited
- **Recommendation**: ⭐⭐⭐⭐

#### Quick Reflexes
- **Effect**: +3% dodge per level
- **Best For**: Agile builds
- **Max Benefit**: Level 15 (50% dodge)
- **Recommendation**: ⭐⭐⭐⭐

### Utility Skills

#### Life Drain
- **Effect**: +10% lifesteal per level
- **Best For**: Sustain builds
- **Max Benefit**: Level 5 (50% lifesteal)
- **Recommendation**: ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

## 🗡️ Equipment System

### Weapon Types
- **Sword**: Balanced damage
- **Axe**: High damage, slower
- **Bow**: Ranged attacks
- **Dagger**: High crit chance
- **Staff**: Magic damage
- **Spear**: Reach advantage
- **Hammer**: AOE potential
- **Scythe**: Lifesteal bonus
- **Katana**: Crit damage bonus

### Rarity Tiers

#### Common (Gray)
- **Drop Rate**: 70%
- **Stats**: 1x multiplier
- **Example**: "Common Sword" - 13 damage at level 5

#### Uncommon (Green)
- **Drop Rate**: 25%
- **Stats**: 1.5x multiplier
- **Example**: "Uncommon Sword" - 19 damage at level 5

#### Rare (Blue)
- **Drop Rate**: 5%
- **Stats**: 2x multiplier
- **Example**: "Rare Sword" - 26 damage at level 5

#### Legendary (Gold)
- **Drop Rate**: Boss drops only
- **Stats**: Unique bonuses
- **Example**: "Shadowfang" - 45 damage + 25% crit

### Level Requirements
- Equipment has minimum level requirements
- You cannot equip items above your level
- Plan your progression accordingly

## 👹 Enemy Types

### Regular Enemies
- **HP**: 50 + (Floor × 20)
- **Damage**: 10 + (Floor × 3)
- **XP**: Floor × 25
- **Loot**: 30% chance for item

### Boss Enemies
- **Appearance**: Every 5 floors (10, 15, 20, 25...)
- **HP**: 2-3x regular enemies
- **Damage**: 1.5-2x regular enemies
- **XP**: Floor × 50
- **Loot**: Guaranteed legendary weapon

## 💡 Pro Tips & Strategies

### Early Game (Floors 1-10)
1. **Focus on Power Strike** - Increases damage immediately
2. **Explore every room** - Don't rush to stairs
3. **Save skill points** - Wait for better understanding
4. **Equip any upgrades** - Even common items help

### Mid Game (Floors 10-20)
1. **Balance offense and defense** - Don't neglect Iron Skin
2. **Prepare for bosses** - Level up before boss floors
3. **Collect legendaries** - They make huge difference
4. **Upgrade Life Drain** - Sustain becomes important

### Late Game (Floors 20+)
1. **Max Critical Eye** - Burst damage is key
2. **High dodge or high defense** - Pick one, max it
3. **Use legendary weapons** - Regular items won't cut it
4. **Full HP before bosses** - Descend stairs to heal

### Build Recommendations

#### Glass Cannon Build
- **Skills**: Power Strike (Max), Critical Eye (Max)
- **Weapon**: High damage (Axe, Greatsword)
- **Strategy**: Kill before being killed
- **Difficulty**: Hard

#### Tank Build
- **Skills**: Iron Skin (Max), Life Drain (Max)
- **Weapon**: Balanced (Sword, Mace)
- **Strategy**: Outlast enemies
- **Difficulty**: Medium

#### Dodge Build
- **Skills**: Quick Reflexes (Max), Critical Eye (High)
- **Weapon**: Fast (Dagger, Katana)
- **Strategy**: Don't get hit
- **Difficulty**: Medium

#### Balanced Build
- **Skills**: Power Strike, Iron Skin, Life Drain (Equal)
- **Weapon**: Any
- **Strategy**: Adapt to situations
- **Difficulty**: Easy

## ❓ FAQ

**Q: Do I lose progress when I die?**
A: Currently, yes. Each run is independent. Future updates may add save systems.

**Q: Can I play with friends?**
A: Yes! Players on the same floor can see each other and play together.

**Q: Is there a level cap?**
A: No! You can level infinitely.

**Q: What's the deepest floor?**
A: There is no limit. The dungeon is infinite.

**Q: Do bosses respawn?**
A: Each floor is unique. Once you descend, you can't go back.

**Q: Can I trade items?**
A: Not yet, but it's planned for future updates.

**Q: How do I heal?**
A: HP fully restores when you descend to a new floor, or use Life Drain skill.

**Q: What happens if I disconnect?**
A: Your character is removed from the game. You'll need to start a new run.
